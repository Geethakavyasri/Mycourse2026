Welcome to Ancient India

India is home to many ancient monuments, temples, caves, historical cities and cultural landmarks.

This webpage presents some of the most fascinating historical places that you can add to your dream travel list.

🌏 My Dream Places

1. 🏛️ Hampi

Hampi is a historical place located in Karnataka. It is famous for its ancient temples, monuments and ruins.

  • State: Karnataka
  • Famous for: Ancient temples and monuments
  • Historical importance: Vijayanagara Empire

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

🔗 Learn More About Hampi

2. 🪨 Ajanta Caves

The Ajanta Caves are ancient rock-cut Buddhist caves located in Maharashtra.

  • State: Maharashtra
  • Famous for: Buddhist paintings
  • Type: Rock-cut caves

⭐ Rating: ★★★★☆

🔗 Learn More About Ajanta

3. 🛕 Ellora Caves

Ellora Caves are famous for their ancient rock-cut temples and monuments.

  • State: Maharashtra
  • Famous for: Kailasa Temple
  • Religions: Hinduism, Buddhism and Jainism

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

🔗 Learn More About Ellora

4. 🏰 Jaipur

Jaipur is a historic city in Rajasthan, famous for its magnificent palaces, forts and traditional architecture.

  • State: Rajasthan
  • Famous for: Amer Fort and City Palace
  • Known as: Pink City

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

🔗 Learn More About Jaipur

5. 🌊 Udaipur

Udaipur is a beautiful historic city in Rajasthan, famous for its lakes, palaces and magnificent architecture.

  • State: Rajasthan
  • Famous for: City Palace and Lake Pichola
  • Known as: City of Lakes

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

🔗 Learn More About Udaipur

6. 🕉️ Kashi

Kashi is one of India's oldest cities, located on the banks of the River Ganga and known for its historic temples and ghats.

  • State: Uttar Pradesh
  • Famous for: Kashi Vishwanath Temple and Ganga Ghats
  • Also known as: Varanasi and Banaras

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

🔗 Learn More About Kashi

7. ☀️ Konark Sun Temple

The Konark Sun Temple is a famous historical temple in Odisha, dedicated to the Sun God.

  • State: Odisha
  • Famous for: Stone architecture
  • Dedicated to: Surya

⭐ Rating: ★★★★★

🔗 Learn More About Konark
